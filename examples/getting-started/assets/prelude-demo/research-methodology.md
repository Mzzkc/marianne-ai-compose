# Research Methodology

How to turn a topic into an outline a writer can draft from without
re-researching:

1. **Start from the reader's tasks**, not the system's structure. List the
   three to five things the audience is trying to do; each becomes a candidate
   section.
2. **For each section, write a 2–3 sentence summary** of what it covers and
   why the reader needs it.
3. **List Key Points** under every section: the facts, rules and edge cases
   the draft must state. A section with fewer than three key points is
   probably two sections merged, or none.
4. **Name prerequisites** — what the reader must already know or have done
   before this section is useful.
5. **Record the audience** in a `### Target Audience` heading at the top,
   restated in your own words from the score's `audience` variable, so the
   writer and reviewer share one picture of the reader.
6. **Order sections by dependency**, then by frequency of need.

The outline is the contract for the draft: headings in the outline become
the draft's `###` sections, in that order.
