# Writing Guidelines

Style the reader can trust across every page:

1. **Second person, present tense.** "You open a connection" — never "the
   user would open".
2. **One idea per paragraph; one example per concept.** A concept without an
   example is a definition, not documentation.
3. **Headings are promises.** `### Overview` says what the page covers;
   `### Summary` says what the reader can now do. Both are required in every
   draft.
4. **Name the failure.** For every operation, say what goes wrong when it is
   misused and what the reader sees when it does.
5. **No marketing register.** "Powerful", "seamless", "simply" do not appear.
6. **Terminology is fixed by the first use.** Define a term once, then reuse
   that exact word; do not vary for elegance.
7. **Code blocks are fenced with a language tag** and are runnable as pasted.
